HouseKit User Manual

Everything HouseKit does, in the order you'll meet it: build a song, shape how it feels, and play along. You don't need to know drum words; HouseKit speaks guitar.

For HouseKit 1.0 on iPad · iPadOS 17 or later, used in landscape

1. Getting started

The first time you open HouseKit, a short guided tour runs on your own starter song. It takes about three minutes and points at the real buttons as you go. Anything it changes can be undone.

  • Skip it any time with Skip tour.
  • Replay it later: tap your song's name at the top of the screen, then Take the Tour.
  • This manual is in the same menu: User Manual.

HouseKit works in landscape only. Headphones or a speaker make the drums sound their best; the iPad's own speakers work fine for getting started.

Quickest start: press Play on the starter song, pick up your guitar, and try the Energy buttons while it plays.

2. The main screen

From top to bottom:

Header
Your song's name (tap it for the song menu), Undo and Redo, the tempo with −, + and TAP, the time signature, your foot pedal status, the kit, and Export. On smaller iPads some of these show as icons only.
Tell HouseKit bar
Type or speak what you want changed, or tap one of the suggestions underneath. See Tell HouseKit.
Song row
Your song as a row of blocks, one per section. Wider blocks have more bars. The selected section has a ring around it, and the one playing fills up as it goes.
Section settings
Everything about the selected section: its name, length, groove, energy and fill.
Transport
Play/Stop, where you are in the song, loop, practice speed, count-in, and Play-along mode.

Show Advanced Controls (in the song menu) adds the drummer's fine-tuning: more feel sliders, time feel, cymbals, time signatures and the Pattern Editor. Everything in this manual works without it unless it says otherwise.

3. Songs

Everything about songs is in the song menu: tap the song's name at the top left.

  • Switch songs: pick one from the list at the top of the menu.
  • New Song gives you four ways to start:
    • Build It for Me… arranges a whole song from a style, a vibe and a length (more).
    • Sounds Like… starts from a real song, a description, or a recording (more).
    • Empty Song starts from nothing.
    • From a template: 14 ready-made layouts, such as a verse–chorus rock song or a 12-bar blues.
  • Rename…, Duplicate and Delete… work on the song that's open.

HouseKit saves as you go; there's no Save button. Songs live on your iPad and are included in its iCloud or computer backups.

Tempo and count-in

  • Use − and + next to the tempo, or tap TAP four or more times in time with the music.
  • The count-in (in the transport) can be none, 1 bar or 2 bars. It plays before the song starts, so you have time to get your hands on the guitar.

Undo and Redo

Undo takes back the last change to the song, up to 50 changes. A slider drag or a knob turn counts as one change. Redo puts back what Undo took away. Undo covers everything that changes the song, including the Kit Editor and Tell HouseKit; the history starts fresh when you switch songs.

4. Sections

A song is made of sections: intro, verse, chorus, bridge, solo, outro, or your own.

  • Select a section: tap its block. Its settings appear below the row.
  • Play from it: double-tap its block.
  • Add one: tap + at the end of the row. It goes after the selected section.
  • Reorder: drag a block left or right.
  • Duplicate or delete: long-press a block, or use the buttons in its settings.

Section settings

Type and name
Tap the type to change it (verse, chorus…), and edit the name if you want something like “Pre-chorus”.
Bars
How long the section is. Use the arrows to change it.
Repeat until I step
The section loops until you move on with Next section, a pedal, or your voice. Use it for solos, jams and vamps whose length changes each time.
Fill into …
The fill the drums play at the end of this section, leading into the next: None, Small or Big.

While a song plays, the selection follows the section you're hearing, so whatever you change is what you're listening to. If you tap another section mid-song, it stays selected until the next section starts.

5. Grooves

Each section has a groove: the pattern the drummer plays. HouseKit has 118, all written by hand, in 12 styles and six time signatures (4/4, 3/4, 5/4, 7/4, 6/8 and 12/8).

  1. Select a section and tap Change groove.
  2. Pick a style tab, or search for a feel such as shuffle, half-time or train beat.
  3. Tap any groove to hear it at your song's tempo.
  4. Tap Use for Verse 1 (or Use for all verses) to keep it, or go back to leave things as they were.

More options in the groove picker sets Simpler, Busier, On the ride or Half-time as you choose it.

A warning appears if your song's tempo is outside the range the groove was written for. It will still play; it may just feel rushed or sleepy.

Time signatures (advanced)

With advanced controls on, each section has a time signature. Changing it picks a groove in the new time from the same style. A song can change time from one section to the next.

6. Energy and feel

Energy is the one control most people need. Pick how big a section feels, and HouseKit chooses the notes, the cymbal and the fills to match:

EnergyWhat you hear
ChillSparse and soft. For quiet verses and intros.
Laid backRelaxed, with room for the guitar.
SteadyThe groove as written.
DrivingBusier and harder, on the ride. For choruses.
BigEverything in, on the crash. For the last chorus.

Changes are heard on the next bar while the song plays. Swing sits under Energy: slide toward Swung for a shuffle feel (it doesn't apply in 6/8 and 12/8, which already swing).

Fine-tune (advanced)

Simpler ↔ Busier
How many notes the drummer adds.
Softer ↔ Harder
How hard the drums are hit.
Locked in ↔ Human
How much the timing and dynamics vary, like a real player.
Time
Half, Normal or Double time feel.
Cymbals
Keep time on the Hi-hat, Ride or Crash.

7. Playing a song

  • Play starts from the selected section, after the count-in. The same button stops; the last hits ring out naturally.
  • Loop repeats the selected section until you turn it off. Great for working on one part.
  • Speed slows the whole song down (to 50%) or speeds it up (to 120%) for practice, without changing the tempo you saved.
  • Every edit is heard live: change a groove, energy or the kit while it plays.

8. Tell HouseKit

Not a drummer? Say what you want in your own words, and HouseKit makes the change and tells you exactly what it did.

  • Type: tap the bar at the top and type a request.
  • Speak: hold the microphone button, say it, and let go. The drums duck while you talk. It works best with headphones.
  • Tap a suggestion under the bar, such as More swing or Punchier.

Things you can ask:

To changeTry saying
Feelmore swing in the chorus, simpler, hit harder, more human
Soundpunchier, bigger room, brighter cymbals, louder kick, use the Big Rusty kit
Groovedifferent groove, a funk groove for the bridge, half time in the outro
Songfaster, tempo 96, add a bridge, add 4 bars to chorus 2, remove the solo, fill into the chorus
Playbackplay, stop, next section, give me a fill, loop this, end it
A new songsomething that sounds like Back in Black opens Sounds Like

Name a section (chorus 2, the bridge, all verses) or leave it out to change the one selected or playing. The reply card has Undo to take the whole request back, and fades after a few seconds.

Voice needs microphone and speech recognition permission (Settings › HouseKit). On iPads that can't recognize speech on the device, it also needs an internet connection.

9. Build It for Me

Song menu › New Song › Build It for Me… (or the button on an empty song) makes a whole arranged song from a few choices:

Style
Rock, blues, funk, country and more.
Count
In 4 (most songs), a waltz (3/4), a rolling 6/8, a slow blues 12/8, or the odd times: in 5 (5/4) or in 7 (7/4).
Vibe
Laid back, Driving or Epic. Epic adds a half-time breakdown.
Tempo
Suggested for the style, or tap along.
Length
Short, Normal or Long.

Tap Build the song. You get calmer verses and bigger choruses, energy that lifts into each chorus and peaks at the last, fills into choruses, and a kit that suits the style. Then change anything you like.

10. Sounds Like

Song menu › New Song › Sounds Like… makes drums after a real song. Three ways in:

  1. Name a song HouseKit knows, such as Back in Black or Billie Jean by Michael Jackson. Tap Songs HouseKit knows to browse all 70.
  2. Describe the feel for anything else: a slow 6/8 blues, fast country shuffle at 140.
  3. Analyze a recording Full: pick an audio file you own (MP3, M4A, WAV or AIFF from the Files app). HouseKit measures its tempo, whether it's straight or shuffled, and whether it's in 3 or 4, then picks its closest groove.

The result shows the tempo, time, style and kit, and how sure HouseKit is. Nudge the tempo if you need to, then:

  • Hear it plays the groove at that tempo.
  • Build the song makes a new song: intro, verses, choruses, a bridge and an outro, with energy rising into the choruses.
  • Save the groove Full adds it to My grooves to use in any song.

Tempos are approximate and the sections are a typical layout, not the record's. Apple Music downloads can't be analyzed, because they're protected.

11. Play-along mode

Tap Play-along mode for a big, simple screen you can read from across the room while your hands are busy.

  • Where you are: the section, the bar and the beat, with a map of the whole song underneath.
  • Countdown: near the end of each section, it shows what's next, beat by beat on the last bar.
  • Transport: previous section, restart section, play/stop, next section, and loop.
  • Side panel: practice speed (±5%), drums volume, a click, and Fill now, Big ending and Tap tempo.

When changes happen

Asking for the next section never breaks the beat. Under When I ask for the next section, choose:

  • After this bar: the drums finish the bar they're in, then move on. Quickest.
  • End of section: the drums play to the end of the section (or this pass of a repeating one), so phrases stay whole.

Tap an action again before it lands to cancel it. Edit song takes you back to the main screen.

12. Foot pedals

Keep your hands on the guitar. HouseKit works with Bluetooth page turners (the kind that act as a keyboard) and MIDI foot controllers (USB or Bluetooth MIDI).

  1. Pair a Bluetooth pedal in the iPad's Settings › Bluetooth, or plug in a MIDI controller.
  2. In HouseKit, tap the pedal chip in the header (or in Play-along mode) to open Foot pedals.
  3. Tap Add a pedal and press the pedal so HouseKit learns it.
  4. Under What each pedal does, choose an action for a tap, a double-tap and a hold.
  5. Use Test it to check each one.

Actions: Play/stop, Next section, Previous section, Restart section, Loop this section, Fill now, Big ending and Tap tempo. You can also have HouseKit flash a banner or play a quiet click in your ears to confirm each press.

13. Kits and the Kit Editor

Tap the kit name in the header to open the Kit Editor. HouseKit has 12 kits: three real recorded drum sets (a jazz club kit, the big rock kit Big Rusty, and a dry vintage kit) shaped in different ways, plus synthesized and electronic kits.

  • Switch kits from the menu at the top.
  • Pick a drum on the left: kick, snare, hi-hat, ride, crash or a tom.
  • Choose its sound Full under Starting sound. Sounds from every kit can be mixed.
  • Shape it: drag a knob up or down; double-tap a knob to reset it. Tone, Room and Volume are always shown; with advanced controls on you also get Tune, Attack, Decay and the drum's character.
  • Hear it: tap to hear the drum, or turn on Hear it in the groove.
  • Save as new kit Full keeps your changes as your own kit for any song.

Edits belong to the song you're in until you save them as a kit; the kit chip shows edited when they differ.

14. The Pattern Editor Full

With advanced controls on, select a section and tap Edit pattern to change its groove note by note.

  • Each row is a drum; each column is a step in the bar.
  • Tapping a step cycles it: off, normal, loud, soft, off.
  • Clear bar empties it to start again.
  • Save as my groove adds it to My grooves, a style tab in the groove picker, to use in any song.

15. Exporting

Tap Export in the header. HouseKit renders the whole song, count-in and ending included, as a 48 kHz, 24-bit stereo WAV that sounds exactly like playback.

  • Save to Files puts it in a folder of your choice. This is the quickest.
  • Share… sends it by AirDrop, Mail, or to another app such as GarageBand.

Every sound in HouseKit is synthesized in the app or released into the public domain, so what you export is yours to use anywhere, including in songs you sell, with no license or credit needed.

16. Free and HouseKit Full

HouseKit is free to download, and the free version is the whole app: building, Energy, Tell HouseKit, Play-along, foot pedals, Undo, export and the tour. It includes:

  • the Rock, Blues & Shuffle, Pop and Ballad styles, and My grooves;
  • the Garage Kit and Big Rusty, with the Kit Editor's knobs;
  • up to 3 songs;
  • Sounds Like songs in those styles.

HouseKit Full is one purchase, not a subscription, and Family Sharing shares it. It adds all 12 styles and 118 grooves, all 12 kits with sound picking and saved kits, unlimited songs, the Pattern Editor, every Sounds Like song, analyzing recordings, and saving grooves. Things marked Full in this manual need it.

  • Every groove and Sounds Like song can be heard before you buy. A lock marks what's in Full; tapping it shows the offer.
  • Unlock or restore any time from the song menu: Unlock HouseKit Full… or Restore Purchases.
  • Nothing you've made is ever locked: songs that use Full grooves or kits keep playing.

17. Tips for practice

  • Learning a tricky part? Loop the section and drop the speed to 70–80%, then raise it 5% at a time.
  • Jamming or soloing? Turn on Repeat until I step and move on with a pedal when you're ready.
  • Don't know the drum words? Describe it: make the verse calmer, more drive in the chorus.
  • Practicing for a gig? Build the set's songs, set each tempo, and step through them in Play-along mode with a pedal.
  • Tight timing practice? Set Locked in (advanced) and turn on the click in Play-along mode.

18. Troubleshooting

No sound

Check the iPad's volume and that the side switch or Control Center isn't muting it, then the drums level in Play-along mode. If another app or a call took the audio, switch away from HouseKit and back. If a banner reports an audio problem, tap Dismiss and press Play again.

The mic doesn't hear me

Turn on Microphone and Speech Recognition in Settings › HouseKit. Hold the mic button the whole time you speak, then let go. On some iPads speech needs an internet connection. You can always type instead.

My pedal doesn't work

Make sure it's paired in Settings › Bluetooth (or plugged in), then add it again in Foot pedals. Page turners need to be in a keyboard mode (often called “page turn” or “arrow keys”); check the pedal's manual.

A recording won't analyze

Use a file you own that the Files app can read: MP3, M4A, WAV or AIFF. Apple Music downloads are protected and can't be read. Songs with a clear drum beat give the best results.

The drums crackle or stutter

Close other apps that use audio, and keep the iPad on power for long sessions. If it keeps happening, email us your iPad model and what was playing.

More answers, and how to reach us, are on the Support page.

19. Words HouseKit uses

Bar
One cycle of the beat: four counts in 4/4, three in 3/4, five in 5/4, seven in 7/4. Sections are measured in bars.
Odd time
A bar of five or seven beats, usually felt in groups such as 3+2 or 4+3. HouseKit's odd-time grooves say their grouping in the groove picker.
BPM
Beats per minute: the tempo.
Count-in
Clicks before the song starts, so you come in together.
Fill
A short drum run at the end of a section that leads into the next.
Groove
The pattern the drummer plays in a section.
Half time
The beat feels half as fast while the tempo stays the same. Heavy and spacious.
Shuffle / swing
Eighth notes played long-short instead of even, as in blues and jazz.
Vamp
A section that repeats until you move on (Repeat until I step).
